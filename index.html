01 / About

Learning from the world.
Building for the world.

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at National Cheng Kung University and a researcher in the Urban Science and Computing Laboratory.

My work asks how machine learning systems can remain useful when the world is heterogeneous, uncertain, and resource-constrained. I connect spatio-temporal modeling, graph learning, multimodal sensing, and systems research to build methods that are both scientifically grounded and operationally meaningful.
