Research thesis

I build machine learning systems for cities, sensing, and sustainable computing.

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at National Cheng Kung University and a researcher in the Urban Science and Computing Laboratory. My work asks how learning systems can remain useful when the world is heterogeneous, uncertain, and resource-constrained.

I connect spatio-temporal modeling, graph learning, multimodal sensing, and systems research to create methods that are scientifically grounded and operationally meaningful.
